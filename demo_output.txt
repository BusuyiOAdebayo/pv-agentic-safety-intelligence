Q: What is the liver safety profile of AX-101?
A: Reversible ALT/transaminase elevations are consistently described across the CTD, RMP, SmPC, USPI and DSUR-style documents. The evidence supports monitoring and a potential-risk classification, but the demo corpus does not establish severe drug-induced liver injury.

Q: Is anaphylaxis an identified risk?
A: Anaphylaxis is not established as an identified risk in the demo corpus. A DSUR case was possibly related but confounded, and the PBRER states causality remains uncertain with ongoing surveillance warranted.

Q: Generate the compound safety risk profile
A: Demonstrates identified/common reactions, potential/monitored liver risk, and anaphylaxis as a signal under evaluation.
