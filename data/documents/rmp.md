# AX-101 Risk Management Plan (synthetic demo)
Document type: RMP
Version: 1.0

## Safety specification
Identified risks: transient nausea and headache observed consistently in clinical development.
Potential risk: hepatotoxicity because reversible ALT elevations occurred in a small subgroup, without Hy's law cases.
Missing information: exposure in severe hepatic impairment and pregnancy.

## Risk minimisation
Routine labeling and laboratory monitoring are considered adequate for nausea, headache, and liver-enzyme elevations.
